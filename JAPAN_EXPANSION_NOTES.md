# Japan intercity expansion — partial coverage

## Implemented

The **Japan** view uses the community Shinkansen timetable dated **10 March 2026**. It contains **927 regular services**, **19 named service families**, **118 station records** and **702 shared timing patterns**, in **333,465 bytes gzip**. The operating corridors are Tōkaidō, Sanyō, Kyūshū, Nishi-Kyūshū, Tōhoku, Hokkaidō, Jōetsu, Hokuriku, Yamagata and Akita, plus represented Gala-Yuzawa services.

Services include Nozomi, Hikari, Kodama, Mizuho, Sakura, Tsubame, Hayabusa, Hayate, Yamabiko, Nasuno, Tsubasa, Komachi, Toki, Tanigawa, Kagayaki, Hakutaka, Asama, Tsurugi and Kamome. This covers the national Shinkansen corridors, **not every extra train or every conventional intercity train**. The unfinished Chūō maglev and Hokkaidō extension are excluded.

The default view also includes **527 JR East limited-express journey sections** reused directly from the existing Tokyo reference, plus **225 JR Kyushu regular limited-express services** in a separate compact reference. Tokyo is dated **20 October 2026**; Kyushu uses **17 March 2026**, the first Tuesday in the operator publication's validity window. All three dates are Tuesdays, and the overlays retain their original local clocks. Both source-date mappings are included in `schedule.source_date_mappings` and explained in the map note. These are separate dated layers, **not claims about simultaneous March 10 operations**. The default combined view has **1,679 services/sections**. No Tokyo timetable is copied into either Japan file. Ordinary JR East locals/rapids and Tokyo subway services are not brought into the national intercity view. The separate Tokyo view remains unchanged.

Transport selects Shinkansen, JR East + Kyushu limited express or both; route and operator filters, details, Overview, fullscreen and themes reuse existing functionality. Filter Operator to **JR Kyushu** and Transport to **Limited express** to focus the addition. Clocks use **Asia/Tokyo**. Source services retain printed clocks, including explicit arrival/departure pairs where published. No passenger loads or live tracking are supplied. Service colours are display colours grouped by train family, not an assertion of official individual route branding. Kyushu uses the operator's red.

## JR Kyushu limited express

The [official operator PDF](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/pdf/timetable_20260314_20270228.pdf) is a 14-page publication explicitly valid **14 March 2026–28 February 2027**; its tables were prepared as of December 2025. On **22 October 2026**, the official URL returned maintenance HTML. The actual PDF was recovered from the [7 March 2026 public archive capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260307062937id_/https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/pdf/timetable_20260314_20270228.pdf). Verified SHA-256: `208a0a10946b7c2b4ddbe9b3eeb19cb1dd8a1425e64d89f91ece743b67405faa`. The script rejects maintenance pages and changed input by hash.

Pages **5–12**, regular **EP** columns, provide **225 selected services across 13 families**: Sonic, Nichirin, Nichirin Seagaia, Hyuga, Kirameki, Kasasagi, Relay Kamome, Midori (including its Relay Kamome variants), Huis Ten Bosch, Yufu, Kyushu Odan Tokkyu, Kaio and Kirishima. Printed Japanese names identify stations; English font fragments inside Japanese parentheses are normalized. Overlapping through-train sections merge by service family/number, preserving consistent arrival/departure clocks. Different named coupled portions retain separate source identities.

**Excluded:** Shinkansen columns (already stored nationally); sightseeing **DS** columns because individual operating calendars and replacement trains have not been validated; and **10 distinct footnote-extended services** whose notes add stations or extend endpoints. Exclusion applies to all matching sections of those trains, avoiding an apparently complete but truncated through service. These are therefore **selected regular services, not complete Kyushu coverage**. In particular, Yufuin no Mori, Aso Boy!, 36+3, Two Stars 4047, A-Train, Ibusuki no Tamatebako and Umisachi Yamasachi are not enabled. Yufu is included. No departures are inferred from headways.

Printed midnight clocks roll into the following day. **Midori 69** retains Hakata **22:35** to Sasebo **24:09**, belongs to its departure day and is not duplicated as another day's occurrence. The replay reference uses shared stop patterns and flat timing arrays, with no per-trip expanded stop arrays.

Prepared timetable: **139,054 bytes gzip**, **91 stations**, **223 timing patterns**, **94 stop patterns**, **234 paths**. All **2,161 service-weighted scheduled legs** have connected paths in the selected snapshot. Preparation graph: **370,336 bytes gzip**, SHA-256 `7df12600ade65e64720abab646992ac494cf3373dd5eda9174e4927b31317dd4`; rebuilding from the pinned PBF produces the same hash. This is approximately **509 KB** of new compressed timetable + preparation geometry, not a raw regional OSM download served to visitors.

Geography is a preparation-only conventional **1067 mm** rail subset of [Geofabrik Kyushu, 20 October 2026](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/japan/kyushu-261020.osm.pbf), SHA-256 `c56764fbe77b66c6dddadc62ebe8f8ea9bb9a0cff8a00f63507c3e731ed88423`. Planned/abandoned tracks, Shinkansen, industrial/test lines and yards are excluded. Station candidates are matched by name near the preserved OSM station catalogue, preferring named stop nodes on the rail graph. Connections use original shared node IDs, with no coordinate-based fabricated junctions. Source station catalogue and its original API provenance are in `data/sources/japan_kyushu_stations.json` and `japan_kyushu_geometry.json`. Raw PBF/PDF/API downloads remain ignored in `.cache/japan`; browsers load only prepared timetable paths. The graph is ODbL 1.0 and is made available alongside the code. It is October geographic reference, not proof of March platform or exact track assignments. Missing paths stay hidden.

Timetable attribution remains **Kyushu Railway Company / JR Kyushu**. This is a factual conversion of the separate operator publication, retaining source rights; no MIT/CC licence is invented for it. The community Shinkansen MIT notice does not apply to this operator timetable. No JR Kyushu station-search/Kotsu database was scraped or imported.

Rebuild with `PYTHONPATH=.cache/pdf-tools ./.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_japan_kyushu_replay.py` after installing preparation-only **pdfplumber** there. It writes `data/reference/japan/limited-express-2026-03-17.json.gz`; `SUNTRAIN_JAPAN_LIMITED_EXPRESS_REFERENCE` overrides it. Geometry rebuild: `PYTHONPATH=.cache/osm-tools ./.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_japan_kyushu_geometry.py .cache/japan/kyushu-261020.osm.pbf` (preparation-only **osmium**). The dated PBF must match its pin. Runtime needs neither package.

## JR West and JR Central — investigated, not enabled

The follow-up request covers Kyushu, JR West and JR Central. **The latter two remain blocked by reusable timetable availability.** Actual official JR West PDFs were downloaded for Haruka, Thunderbird, Yakumo, Sunrise Seto/Izumo and commuter limited expresses. They carry **Kotsu Shimbunsha** rights; their online [dated train timetable](https://timetable.jr-odekake.net/train-timetable/28011?date=20261020) explicitly prohibits unauthorized republication, copying or processing into other media. The page supplies Central's Nanki as well as West services, so this restriction cannot be avoided by selecting another operator there. No West PDF/online train schedule has been added to the public dataset.

JR Central's [station-timetable page](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/time-schedule/search/) was checked; it is not a validated downloadable complete conventional feed. The accessible JR East **Shinano 1** page also identifies the October Kotsu Shimbunsha database, not a separate open national source. The [Kotsu bulk-data service](https://www.kotsu.co.jp/service/jikoku_solution/data_sales/) is a credible complete-data route but requires a commercial agreement and explicit replay redistribution terms. No payment, registration, contract or external contact was made. Tourist route maps, undated summaries and advertised headways have not been substituted for schedules. Next time: obtain a permitted dated feed or operator-owned publication before converting these groups.

## Timetable provenance and duplication

Downloaded and inspected: [Shinkansen Live `timetable/data.json`](https://github.com/manavya-des/shinkansen-live-open/blob/2e0cd13e5a60dd01238dc4f6ebf41a967eb85d96/timetable/data.json), from immutable revision `2e0cd13e5a60dd01238dc4f6ebf41a967eb85d96` (11 March 2026). The data explicitly declares `date=2026-03-10`; it is a community dataset, not a national official GTFS archive. Raw timetable SHA-256: `73ed3276be89dc846237fe1dd588d3606fedee557c7983bbc0e20fa6bba1dcaa`.

Only rows explicitly marked daily or weekday and without caution symbols are selected for this Tuesday. **193 source rows** with blank/conditional operation information are excluded, rather than inventing their operating dates. Blank and arrow cells do not create timed station calls. Tōkaidō/Sanyō/Kyūshū overlapping columns are merged by service name, number and direction, retaining the union of consistent printed stops. A conflicting duplicate fails preparation. Nozomi 1 appears once from Tokyo **06:00** to Hakata **10:52**, including its intermediate calls; it is not emitted again at Shin-Osaka.

The station catalogue is extracted from the same revision's [map station table](https://github.com/manavya-des/shinkansen-live-open/blob/2e0cd13e5a60dd01238dc4f6ebf41a967eb85d96/src/components/map-client.tsx) and [Japanese/English names](https://github.com/manavya-des/shinkansen-live-open/blob/2e0cd13e5a60dd01238dc4f6ebf41a967eb85d96/src/app/timetable/page.tsx), in `data/sources/japan_shinkansen_stations.json`. Japanese identifiers distinguish **仙台** in Tōhoku from **川内** in Kyūshū even though both translate to Sendai. Mikawa-Anjō's source point was on the conventional station rather than the Shinkansen station; its corrected coordinate comes from [OSM station node 6620847242](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6620847242), recorded in that catalogue.

## Connected railway geometry

The community project's display polylines contained multi-kilometre shortcuts and were **not used** in the final replay. SunTrain downloaded the original operating railway ways from ten [OpenStreetMap API](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/API_v0.6) route relations on **21 October 2026**. Exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `data/sources/japan_shinkansen_geometry.json`. Nodes connect only by their original OSM IDs; platforms and construction ways are excluded. The existing railway graph and 150 m station-matching rule are reused, then paths are simplified for playback. This is inferred railway alignment, not proof of a historical running track or platform.

The preparation-only graph is **790,361 bytes gzip**, SHA-256 `05fc1fc57f4b43c9267a81c31af1f948d505191a2abd6f0235356128703d6c68`. **8,218 of 8,358 scheduled journey legs (98.3%)** have connected paths. This weights legs by included services; it is not a fraction of physical railway mileage. Three underlying station sections remain unresolved: Kurikoma-Kōgen–Ichinoseki, Morioka–Iwate-Numakunai and Echigo-Yuzawa–Gala-Yuzawa. A timed express leg crossing one of these remains hidden until its next supported station. Nozomi 1's entire journey has mapped paths.

## Remaining conventional intercity coverage

**The request for all Japanese intercity trains is not complete.** No complete reusable national conventional timetable was validated during this investigation. Do not label the JR East + selected Kyushu overlay as nationwide conventional coverage. Important remaining groups include JR Central's conventional limited expresses, JR West's wider limited-express network and Sunrise sleepers, JR Hokkaidō's conventional services, JR Shikoku, remaining Kyushu services and private intercity operators such as Kintetsu.

Validated source leads:

- [Kotsu Shimbunsha bulk timetable data](https://www.kotsu.co.jp/service/jikoku_solution/data_sales/) explicitly offers JR passenger companies' nationwide data in CSV/XML/GTFS, plus private railway data. It is a commercial service for corporations/organisations, not an unrestricted downloadable feed. Access and permission to redistribute prepared replay data need confirmation; no subscription or contract was purchased.
- [JR Kyūshū's actual 14-page timetable PDF](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/pdf/timetable_20260314_20270228.pdf) declares **14 March 2026–28 February 2027**. Selected regular columns are now converted as described above. Remaining sightseeing calendars, replacement trains and footnote extensions still need work. Its official URL currently serves maintenance HTML; use the verified archived PDF. It must not be presented as a March 10 timetable.
- [JR Hokkaidō's timetable/route page](https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/train/index.html) links station-name PDFs and timetable search. The accessible [MajorTrainTimetable.pdf](https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/pdf/MajorTrainTimetable.pdf) is explicitly **2022**, so it cannot establish 2026 service. Search results are not a validated downloadable national archive.
- [JR Shikoku station timetables](https://www.jr-shikoku.co.jp/01_trainbus/jikoku/index.html) are available as references; complete dated train-level conversion and reuse terms remain unvalidated.
- [JR Central's timetable page](https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/timetable/) supplies dated basic Shinkansen PDFs and explicitly says they omit some extra trains. It does not establish a complete conventional intercity feed.
- The [ODPT JR East catalogue](https://ckan.odpt.org/en/dataset/jreast_tokyo_area) is the previously investigated restricted Kantō feed, not nationwide JR or Shinkansen coverage. A Challenge token alone does not solve this national-data gap.

Next step for full conventional coverage: secure a suitable dated bulk source with reuse terms, or validate and convert operator publications individually. Preserve actual calendars, overnight times, all printed stops, and operator-specific paths; never create departures from advertised headways. Passenger data remains optional.

## Rebuild

Run `./.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_japan_replay.py`. It verifies the immutable timetable and bundled graph hashes and writes `data/reference/japan/2026-03-10.json.gz`. Raw downloads remain in ignored `.cache/japan/`. `SUNTRAIN_JAPAN_REFERENCE` overrides the prepared timetable.

To rebuild the graph from the preserved raw OSM responses, run `./.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_japan_geometry.py`. OSM is mutable: if a fresh response differs, inspect it before updating source/compiled hashes. The graph is not opened by the website at runtime. Expanding dates requires actual timetable/calendar evidence; changing the date picker alone is insufficient. Keep the JR East weekday mapping labelled and preserve the single stored Tokyo reference.
