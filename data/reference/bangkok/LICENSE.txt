SunTrain changes: Bangkok rail selection; English display names from the feed;
invalid and duplicate template exclusions; source calendar/holiday selection;
illustrative departures from headway windows; shared stop/timing representation;
ordered projection and clipping of each trip's own supplied railway shape.

These are frequency-based journey estimates and sections, not individual train
observations, live positions or passenger counts. Source colours and selected
station times are retained. No national SRT placeholder schedules are replayed.
Unmatched Red Line station approaches remain gaps; no straight links are inserted.
