Railway geometry and station coordinates
=======================================
Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors. Licensed under ODbL 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The connected geometry database, derived railway paths and station
coordinates retain ODbL terms.
Original OSM relation URLs and raw source hashes are recorded in
shinkansen-tracks-2026-10-21.json.gz and data/sources/japan_shinkansen_geometry.json.
Mikawa-Anjo coordinates use OSM node 6620847242; the catalogue records this.
The MIT notice does not relicense OSM data or other operators' data.

SunTrain selects printed regular services, deduplicates overlapping timetable
sections and matches stops to connected railway ways. It uses no official
logos, reservation data, proprietary map tiles or live-status endpoints.
The existing Tokyo reference has its own notice and is reused without copying.

JR KYUSHU LIMITED EXPRESS ADDITION

limited-express-2026-03-17.json.gz derives selected factual stop clocks from
Kyushu Railway Company / JR Kyushu's published operator timetable, valid
14 March 2026 to 28 February 2027. Original source rights are retained;
the Shinkansen community MIT licence above does not apply to this publication.
Source: https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/pdf/timetable_20260314_20270228.pdf
Archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20260307062937id_/https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/english/pdf/timetable_20260314_20270228.pdf
SunTrain selected regular services, excluded unverified sightseeing calendars
and footnote-extended journeys, merged overlapping sections, normalized names,
mapped railway paths and compacted schedules. No open operator licence is
asserted. The date mapping into the national view retains this source date.

Kyushu railway paths, station catalogue and preparation graph:
Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Dated source: https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/japan/kyushu-261020.osm.pbf
The derived database is kyushu-tracks-2026-10-22.json.gz, prepared October 22
from the October 20 extract. Station seed API sources and their hashes are
preserved in data/sources/japan_kyushu_geometry.json.
