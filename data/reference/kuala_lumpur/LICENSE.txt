Changes by SunTrain: KL rail selection, shared stop/timing conversion, illustrative
departures from Rapid KL headways, railway graph extraction, station matching and
path simplification. Source timetable times and route colours are retained.
ERL departures are converted from explicit PDF rows, not generated from headways.
Combined late-night airport services are stored once under Transit; intermediate
station events retain the printed times without invented dwell/arrival times.
ERL trip IDs are SunTrain identifiers, not published train or vehicle numbers.
Rapid KL estimates are not actual individual departures or live observations.
Source URLs, dates and SHA-256 hashes are retained in the data and expansion notes.
