Source dates of operation are unconfirmed. Replay dates in April 2023 are
illustrative. Original clocks, scheduled calls and overnight offsets are retained.
SunTrain selects conventional passenger numbers 1–499 and uses the source origin's
Daily value as a working calendar; this is not a verified historical service calendar.
Operational stops can appear in this working timetable; passenger loads are absent.
